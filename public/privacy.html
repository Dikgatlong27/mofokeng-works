Your privacy matters

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Mofokeng Works mobile application handles information when you request vehicle diagnostic, repair, or key services.

Effective date: 13 August 2026

At a glance

No accountYou do not need to register or sign in.
No advertisingThe app does not display third-party ads.
You stay in controlYou choose when to send a quote request.
01

Who we are

Mofokeng Works provides automotive diagnostic, repair, servicing, and vehicle-key services. This Privacy Policy applies to the Mofokeng Works mobile application (the “App”).

02

Information you choose to provide

When requesting a quote, you may enter or select:

  • Your name and telephone number
  • Your vehicle model
  • Vehicle symptoms or problems
  • The services you are interested in

Please do not include information that is not needed for us to respond to your request.

03

How the App uses information

The App formats the details you provide into a quote-request message. When you tap “Send via WhatsApp,” the App opens WhatsApp so that you can review and send the message to Mofokeng Works. We use the information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and prepare a quote
  • Understand the vehicle issue or requested service
  • Contact you about the request and arrange service
  • Maintain business records where required by law
04

Sharing and third-party services

The App does not sell your personal information. Quote requests are sent only when you choose to continue through WhatsApp. WhatsApp is operated by a third party, and its processing of information is governed by its own privacy policy and terms. Your mobile network or internet provider may also process connection data.

05

Storage, retention, and security

The App does not create a Mofokeng Works user account or operate its own cloud database for quote requests. After you send a request, the message may remain in the WhatsApp conversation on your device and ours. We keep enquiry information only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, provide services, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

06

Children’s privacy

The App is intended for vehicle owners and customers aged 18 or older. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

07

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by Mofokeng Works. You may also object to or restrict certain processing. Email us using the address below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

08

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our practices, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
